In an anisotropic crystal the permittivity is a tensor and a given direction supports two waves with different speeds. In a dispersive medium a wave packet travels at the group velocity, not the phase velocity. In a conductor free charges make the wave number complex: the wave is damped within a skin depth and is almost entirely reflected.
