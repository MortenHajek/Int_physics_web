Concepts and equationsPojmy a rovnice

The Euler–Lagrange equations can rarely be solved directly, but two general properties of the Lagrangian integrate them in part without solving them. If does not contain a coordinate , the corresponding generalized momentum is constant; if does not contain time explicitly, the energy integral is constant. Each such statement is a first integral of the equations of motion — a differential equation of first order in place of one of second order.Eulerovy–Lagrangeovy rovnice lze přímo vyřešit jen zřídka, dvě obecné vlastnosti Lagrangeovy funkce je však umožňují zčásti integrovat, aniž by se řešily. Neobsahuje-li souřadnici , je příslušná zobecněná hybnost konstantní; neobsahuje-li explicitně čas, je konstantní integrál energie. Každé takové tvrzení je prvním integrálem pohybových rovnic — diferenciální rovnicí prvního řádu namísto rovnice řádu druhého.

Behind both lies symmetry. The Lagrangian of an isolated system does not change when the system is shifted in time, shifted in space or rotated, and these three symmetries — homogeneity of time, homogeneity of space and isotropy of space — give the conservation of energy, of total momentum and of angular momentum. Emmy Noether’s theorem (1915) states the general rule: every continuous symmetry of the Lagrangian is accompanied by a conserved quantity.Za oběma stojí symetrie. Lagrangeova funkce izolované soustavy se nezmění, posune-li se soustava v čase, posune-li se v prostoru nebo pootočí-li se, a tyto tři symetrie — homogenita času, homogenita prostoru a izotropie prostoru — dávají zákon zachování energie, celkové hybnosti a momentu hybnosti. Obecné pravidlo vyslovuje teorém Emmy Noetherové (1915): s každou spojitou symetrií Lagrangeovy funkce souvisí nějaká zachovávající se veličina.

Its equation of motion says that its momentum keeps its initial value, and this statement, containing at most first derivatives, is a partial integration of the equation of motion. Homogeneity of space — momentumHomogenita prostoru — hybnost

If shifting every particle by the same vector leaves unchanged, the sum of the derivatives vanishes and, by the equations of motion, the total momentum of the system is conserved. Its centre of mass then moves uniformly in a straight line whatever the internal forces, and for two particles : the law of action and reaction is a consequence of the homogeneity of space. A system symmetric only about one axis — in a uniform gravitational field, on a cone — conserves only the component along that axis. Noether’s theorem contains all three cases: to every continuous symmetry of the Lagrangian belongs a conserved quantity.
