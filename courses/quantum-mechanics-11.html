The Stern–Gerlach experiment, the Zeeman effect and the fine structure of spectral lines showed that an electron has a property none of the quantities of classical physics describes: an intrinsic angular momentum, spin, with a magnetic moment associated with it. Its projection onto any axis takes only the two values .
