The harmonic oscillatorHarmonický oscilátor

Equally spaced levels above the zero-point energyEkvidistantní hladiny nad energií nulových kmitů

Concepts and equationsPojmy a rovnice

Near the minimum of any smooth potential the potential is a parabola, so the harmonic oscillator describes small vibrations of every kind — atoms in molecules and crystals and, as a set of independent oscillators, the electromagnetic field. It is one of the few problems solved exactly. The text solves the oscillator in wave mechanics, where the eigenfunctions are Gaussians multiplied by Hermite polynomials. The same spectrum follows from the commutator alone, without choosing a representation: the Hamiltonian factorises into a product of a lowering and a raising operator, which step from one level to the next.
