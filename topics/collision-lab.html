Collision Lab

Slide two bodies on an airless track. Dial the coefficient of restitution from 0 (fully inelastic) to 1 (perfectly elastic) and watch momentum hold while energy doesn't.

What this teaches

three core ideas

Elastic limit

e = 1: both momentum and kinetic energy conserved. Perfect rebound.

Inelastic limit

e = 0: bodies stick. ΔKE goes to heat. Watch it on the energy bar.

Impulse arrows

Force × dt rendered live during contact — the area is the change in momentum.
