What this teaches

three core ideas

Inverse-square law

F ∝ 1/r² — double the separation, quarter the force. Drag to feel it.

Superposition

The total field at any point is the vector sum of contributions from each charge.

Field lines & equipotentials

Lines are always perpendicular to equipotentials. Dense lines mean strong field.
