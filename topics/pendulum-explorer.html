What this teaches

three core ideas

Small-angle limit

For θ ≪ 1, sin θ ≈ θ and the period reduces to 2π√(L/g).

Phase space

Trajectory in (θ, θ̇). Bounded for small energy, unbounded above the separatrix.

Double pendulum

Two coupled DOF, chaotic. Tiny changes in θ₀ explode over a few swings.
