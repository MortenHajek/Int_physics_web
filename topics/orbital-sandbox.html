What this teaches

three core ideas

Conic sections

Circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola — all from a single initial velocity.

Kepler's 2nd

Equal areas in equal times, shaded live as the orbit progresses.

Energy & escape

Total E switches sign at escape velocity. Watch the orbit unbind.
